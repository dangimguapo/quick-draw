# Project rules

Mobile game, phone **landscape** only. 2–3 players, online matchmaking with accounts. All ages — must read clearly for kids without feeling babyish. Copy tone: playful cartoon.

## Locked design decisions
- Combat screen: **2b** — single fan of circles at the bottom, FIRE circles at the far ends leaning toward their target, each rival owning a colour carried onto their FIRE circle and the floor behind them.
- Character select: **4a** — roster grid left (all six visible, no scroll), blown-up hero + one-sentence power right.
- Elimination: **5a** (beat never pauses, loser drains in place, circles slide inward). Victory: **5b** (winner in their colour, three plain numbers, REMATCH loud).
- Tutorial: **7a** swipe cards on first launch; **7c** training is a repeatable choice on the home screen; **7b** cheat sheet behind pause.
- Home screen: **8a** — one loud PLAY, Training as a peer beneath it.
- Approved title: *Quick Draw*.

## Game rules
- Beat = 2s: 1s to decide, 1s to resolve. Reveal all icons, then animate outcomes. No pauses between beats.
- 3 hearts each; start with 0 bullets. Reload = +1 shot, unlimited, but never protects you. Block is total. Fire costs 1 shot.
- Special power: once per round, recharges next round. **Using a power is your whole action for that beat** — only Double Shield protects you.
- Opponents' hearts are visible; their bullet counts are never shown.
- **A match locks when it starts.** No joining in progress; an empty 3rd seat stays empty.
